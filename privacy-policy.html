Privacy Policy

Undead Rising for Windows

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Undead Rising is an offline, single-player game. The game stores progress and preferences on your device and does not send them to Nova Forge. No in-game account, advertising or integrated analytics service is included in this version.

1. Who this policy covers

Nova Forge publishes Undead Rising. This policy describes data handling by the Windows version of the game and privacy-related communications you send to Nova Forge. Microsoft Store, Windows and other services you choose to use have their own privacy practices.

2. Information stored on your device

The game uses local files to provide and restore your experience. These may include:

  • Saved progress, checkpoints, backup saves, story choices, endings, inventory and character state.
  • Character appearance, graphics, audio, accessibility and control preferences.
  • Local diagnostic logs produced by the game engine, which may contain technical information such as the renderer, graphics device or error messages.

These files are used to run the game, remember your choices and help diagnose problems. The game does not automatically upload these files to Nova Forge. Device backup or synchronization software you separately enable may copy them according to that software's settings.

3. Accounts, tracking and sharing

Version 1.0.0 has no in-game registration, Google login, multiplayer, cloud-save service, advertising SDK or integrated analytics or crash-report upload service. The game does not request your name, email address, contacts or precise location, and does not access your camera or microphone.

Nova Forge does not sell local game data or use it for targeted advertising. The game does not transmit your local progress or preferences to advertising companies or other external services.

4. Microsoft Store and Windows

Microsoft may process account, purchase, licensing, installation, usage or diagnostic information when you use Microsoft Store, Windows or related platform services. This processing is separate from the game's local save system and is governed by Microsoft's terms, privacy statement and your platform settings.

The game does not provide Nova Forge with your Microsoft account password or payment-card details. Microsoft may make Store performance or diagnostic reports available to the publisher. If Nova Forge uses those reports, they are used to understand game distribution and address quality or reliability issues.

For details and available controls, read the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

5. Information you send for support

If you email Nova Forge, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your message, screenshots or diagnostic logs. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

Support messages are processed through our email provider. We retain them for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, related follow-up and applicable legal obligations. Necessary information may be processed by the email provider or disclosed where required by law.

6. Your choices and deletion

You control the local game files on your device. You can remove saved progress and preferences by deleting the game's local user-data files while the game is closed. Deleting these files removes the associated progress or settings; keep a backup first if you want to retain them. Uninstalling the game may leave local files or separately maintained backups behind.

For help locating these files, or to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent directly to Nova Forge, contact the email address below. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location. Nova Forge cannot delete Microsoft account or platform records; use Microsoft's privacy controls for those records.

7. Children

The game does not include a personal-information collection form or in-game account for children. Please follow the game's published age rating. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Nova Forge through support, contact us to request its removal.

8. Policy changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the game or its data practices. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Any future online features will require an updated explanation of their data handling.
